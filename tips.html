Simple Eco-Friendly Tips 🌱

Practical ideas to help you make more sustainable choices every day.

Tips for Greener Living

Choose the habits that work for your lifestyle and circumstances.

TIP 01
💧

Save Water

Turn off taps when not needed and report leaking taps when possible.

TIP 02
💡

Save Electricity

Switch off unused lights, fans and other electrical devices.

TIP 03
🛍️

Use Reusable Bags

Carry a reusable bag when shopping to reduce disposable plastic use.

TIP 04
🌳

Care for Plants

Grow suitable plants and care for green spaces in your community.

TIP 05
🚌

Travel Thoughtfully

When practical and safe, walk, cycle or use public transport.

TIP 06
🍎

Avoid Food Waste

Plan meals, store food properly and use leftovers safely.

TIP 07
♻️

Reuse and Recycle

Reuse suitable items and separate waste according to local recycling rules.

TIP 08
📚

Spread Awareness

Share reliable environmental information with friends and family.

TIP 09
🧴

Choose Less Packaging

When practical, choose products with less unnecessary packaging.
